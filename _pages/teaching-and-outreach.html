I believe that sharing scientific knowledge is an essential part of research, and I enjoy communicating my work to a wide range of audiences. Below is a selected list of teaching and outreach activities in which I have participated.

Outreach

I had the pleasure of delivering an invited public lecture at the Forum des Sciences in Villeneuve-d'Ascq, France. This was a unique opportunity to share my research and introduce high-energy astrophysics to a non-specialist audience of all ages.

I have also participated in various outreach events, including the 2022 Science Fair and Astronomy Night at the Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris, and the 2024 Particules du cosmos Masterclass at the Interuniversity Institute for High Energies in Brussels.

Beyond communicating scientific results, I enjoy sharing my personal experience and encouraging students interested in pursuing a career in astrophysics. Over the years, I have had several opportunities to discuss my academic journey with high school students visiting the Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris, as well as with students at Lycée Arago in Paris, where I completed my secondary education.
