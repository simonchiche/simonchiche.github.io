I believe that sharing scientific knowledge is an essential part of research, and I enjoy communicating my work to a wide range of audiences and I am always open to teach or participate to outreach activites.

Outreach

I had the pleasure of delivering an invited public lecture at the Forum des Sciences in Villeneuve-d'Ascq, France. This was a unique opportunity to discuss my research and advertise high-energy astrophysics to non-specialists people from all ages.

I also engaged in activities such as the 2022 science fair and astronomy night at Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris, and the 2024 Materclass “Particules du cosmos” at the Interuniversity Institute for High Energies.

I also enjoy sharing my experience and offering guidance to students interested in pursuing a career in astrophysics. Over the years, I have had several opportunities to discuss my academic journey with high school students visiting the Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris, as well as with students at Lycée Arago in Paris, where I completed my secondary education.
